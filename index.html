How to build

  • Drag an atom from the tray, or tap it to drop one in.
  • Drop atoms next to each other to bond. Amber dots are electrons ready to bond; white dot pairs are lone pairs.
  • Metals (Li, Na, K, Mg) don't share electrons. Drop one by a nonmetal and it hands its electrons over, making ions.
  • Tap an atom to open its electron panel: its Lewis dot structure, plus buttons to add or remove electrons. It stays open while you build.
  • Tap a bond to remove one line from it: triple becomes double, double becomes single, and a single bond breaks. Use New bonds: Single for carbon chains.
  • Closing a ring? Tap one atom, press Bond to another atom, then tap the other.
  • Duplicate (or Ctrl+D) copies the molecule you last tapped.
  • Fill with H caps every open spot on the selected molecule with hydrogen. Build the carbon skeleton first, then fill.
  • If a molecule isn’t finished, the readout says which atoms still need something.
  • Drag an atom and its molecule follows. Drop it on the bin to remove it. Undo or Ctrl+Z takes back a change.
